    Basic Security

    Permission-Based Self-Healing (production devices)

    Off by default. When on, an approved change can be executed on a real-network device by an account whose group holds “Execute approved changes on production devices”, never by the approver. NetGuard backs up the configuration, arms the device's own revert timer where the platform has one, verifies the rules, the required flows on the digital twin and live reachability, and rolls back automatically unless the change is confirmed in time. Only a confirmed change is saved.
